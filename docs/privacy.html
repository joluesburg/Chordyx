Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Chordyx (“the App”) is developed by Jose L. Espinosa Burgos (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Summary

Chordyx is designed to work primarily on your device and between nearby devices over local Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth. Chordyx does not require a Chordyx account, does not sell your personal information, and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Optional Internet join-code backup and optional library backup use your Apple iCloud (CloudKit) when you enable them — not a Chordyx-operated user database.

Information the App stores on your device

The App may store the following data on your device:

  • Saved chord progressions — names, keys, chords, and related settings you choose to save
  • Display name — the name you enter so other musicians can identify you in a session (defaults to your device name if you do not change it)
  • App preferences — such as metronome volume, notation choice, and other in-app settings

This data stays in the App’s local storage unless you delete it, remove the App, or turn on optional iCloud backup for progressions (when available in Settings).

Live sessions with other musicians

When you host or join a session, Chordyx uses Apple’s Multipeer Connectivity framework to discover and connect with nearby devices over local Wi‑Fi and/or Bluetooth. During a session, chord progressions, tempo, piano notes, and related session data are shared directly between connected devices.

That local communication is peer-to-peer. It does not pass through Chordyx servers. Apple’s system may encrypt this connection using encryption built into iOS/iPadOS/macOS.

The App requests permission to use the local network so nearby sessions can be discovered.

Optional Internet join backup (iCloud)

If the host enables Internet backup, Chordyx can publish a short-lived session record to the host’s Apple iCloud (CloudKit) so guests can join with a 6-character code over cellular or when venue Wi‑Fi is unreliable. Both host and guest must be signed in to iCloud. Session records are temporary join metadata and live session sync for that code — not a public Chordyx social network.

Chordyx does not operate a separate login system. Access is gated by Apple’s iCloud account on the device.

MIDI and audio

If you connect a MIDI keyboard or use the in-app piano, note data is processed on your device and may be shared with connected session participants while a session is active. Metronome audio plays locally on your device. If you allow microphone access for optional practice features, audio is processed on your device and is not uploaded to Chordyx servers.

Information we do not collect

  • No Chordyx account registration or Chordyx login is required
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs are built into the App
  • No payment information is collected by us through the App
  • We do not receive your saved progressions or session content on Chordyx-operated servers

Third-party services

Distribution of the App through Apple’s App Store and TestFlight is subject to Apple’s privacy practices. We do not control Apple’s processing of data related to app downloads, crash reports you choose to share with Apple, TestFlight feedback you submit to Apple, or iCloud/CloudKit storage tied to your Apple ID when you enable those features.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Your choices

  • You can delete saved progressions inside the App
  • You can change or clear your display name in the App
  • You can leave a session at any time
  • You can disable Internet join backup when hosting
  • You can uninstall the App to remove locally stored data
  • You can manage iCloud data for the App in Apple device Settings

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
