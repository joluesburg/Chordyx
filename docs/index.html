Chordyx

Share chord progressions in real time with your band — on stage, in rehearsal, or at home.

What it does

  • Host or join a live session with nearby musicians
  • Follow chords on a circular progression display
  • Sync metronome, piano, and fretboard views
  • Save progressions and build setlists on the fly
  • Support chord symbols and Solfège notation

Beta testing

Chordyx is currently in TestFlight beta. You need two devices on the same Wi‑Fi (or nearby via Bluetooth) to test host and guest sessions together.
